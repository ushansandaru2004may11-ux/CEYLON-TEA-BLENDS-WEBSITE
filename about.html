Our Heritage

The story behind the world's finest cup of tea.

A Legacy Since 1867

Ceylon Tea Blends was born out of a deep passion for Sri Lanka's rich tea-making traditions. What started over a century ago in the central highlands of Ceylon continues today with the same dedication to quality and flavor.

Our tea leaves are strictly hand-picked by expert tea pluckers. We ensure that only the tender "two leaves and a bud" are selected, guaranteeing the exquisite aroma and robust flavor that genuine Ceylon Tea is famous for worldwide.
