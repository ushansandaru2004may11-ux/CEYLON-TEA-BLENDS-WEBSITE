Chronicles of Paradise

From Loolkandura to Buckingham Palace

1867

James Taylor Plants Field No. 7

A 32-year-old Scotsman named James Taylor plants 19 acres of tea seedlings at Loolkandura Estate in Kandy, Sri Lanka. Working on the verandah of his bungalow, he hand-rolls the leaves and crafts Ceylon's very first commercial batch.

1
1873

First Export to London Auction

The first consignment of 23 pounds of pure Ceylon tea arrives at the Mincing Lane London tea auction. British merchants are spellbound by its luminous reddish-amber liquor and crisp briskness, launching a worldwide sensation.

2
1893

Chicago World's Columbian Exposition

Over one million packages of Ceylon tea are sold at the World’s Fair in Chicago. Ceylon establishes its reputation as the global benchmark for orthodox, unblended whole-leaf black tea.

3
🦁 LEGAL TRADEMARK OF SRI LANKA TEA BOARD

The Sacred Ceylon Lion Logo Standard

The sword-bearing Ceylon Lion is not merely a label—it is the world’s most strictly monitored single-country origin guarantee. To carry this logo, every harvest must satisfy three non-negotiable statutory requirements:

  • 1.
    100% Pure Ceylon Cultivation The tea must be cultivated and hand-plucked exclusively on tea estates situated on the island of Sri Lanka. No blending with lower-grade teas from other nations is permitted.
  • 2.
    100% Packed in Sri Lanka To prevent adulteration during shipping, the tea must be packed in certified airtight containers prior to departure from Colombo.
  • 3.
    Rigorous Organoleptic Testing Every batch undergoes sensory cupping by official government master tea tasters to guarantee liquor color, aroma, and tannin purity.
🦁

17 Arrowheads on Sword

Notice the sword held by the lion: precisely 17 arrowheads represent the 17 quality parameters monitored by the Sri Lanka Tea Board. Look for this hallmark on all our packages.

Our Pledge

World’s First Ozone Friendly Tea

Sri Lanka is the only nation on Earth where 100% of tea cultivation is completely free of Methyl Bromide fumigation, recognized under the Montreal Protocol. When you drink Ceylon Royale, you protect the ozone layer, conserve elephant wildlife corridors, and support healthcare and housing for our female tea plucker communities.
