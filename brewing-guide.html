💧

Never Boil Water Twice

Re-boiling exhausts dissolved oxygen from the water. Oxygen is the vessel that transports the delicate volatile aromatic oils from the tea leaf to your palate. Always draw fresh, cold water.

🫖

Pre-Warm The Teapot

Swirl boiling water inside your porcelain or clay pot and discard it before adding leaves. A cold pot can drop the water temperature by up to 10°C, causing under-extraction of tea polyphenols.

⏱️

Do Not Squeeze The Bag

Squeezing the infuser or tea bag releases bitter hydrolyzable tannins into the brew. Simply lift gently and allow gravity to drain the golden nectar naturally.
